App name (max 30):
Not a Fake Ad: Choices

Short description (max 80):
Save the guy from the ads! Pick the right item or watch the funny fail.

Full description:
You know those ads: a guy hanging over lava, two items below, and he always picks the silliest one. This time, you choose.

Not a Fake Ad: Choices is the "pick the right item" puzzle from the ads, made actually playable. Look at the danger and tap the item that saves him before time runs out. Pick wrong? Every wrong item has its own funny ending.

• 50 dangers: lava, sharks, dragons, vampires, tornadoes, zombies and more
• Space, Ocean and City packs
• Trick dangers: the obvious answer isn't always right
• 150 different funny endings to collect in your Fail Album
• A boss every 25 levels, plus bonus rounds
• Endless mode and a daily challenge that's the same for everyone
• Challenge a friend: send your score and let them try to beat it on the same dangers
• A 24-card collection, outfits, pets, a lucky wheel and chests
• Weekly leaderboards, achievements and weekly events
• Your progress is safe in your account, even on a new phone
• English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French

The ad lied. This one doesn't.
